# This is NOT the Next.js you know

This version has breaking changes — APIs, conventions, and file structure may all differ from your training data. Read the relevant guide in `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` before writing any code. Heed deprecation notices.
<!-- END:nextjs-agent-rules -->

## Product language

- The current Curipo product is English-only.
- All user-facing website copy and all database content must be written in English.
- Do not add mixed-language or localized content yet. Additional languages will be introduced in a later localization phase.

## Current MVP direction

- Curipo V0 is a personal AI agent card binder, not a marketplace, social network, or game.
- Agents should feel memorable and personable, like collectible business or sports cards with clear service specialties.
- The core loop is: discover an agent, save its card locally, generate a shareable page or social card, and refer it to someone else.
- Prioritize iMessage link previews and exportable Instagram-friendly share images.
- Do not add user accounts, friends, UGC, messaging, payments, or direct agent execution in V0.
